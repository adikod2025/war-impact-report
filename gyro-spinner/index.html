Gyro Spinner

Spin your iPhone on its axis — the gyroscope fires the shutter at perfectly even angles for a full 360° set.

Shots per 360°

24 shots suits most photogrammetry / turntable sets. Use 36–72 for stitched panoramas.

Spin drive

The phone throws its own mass around: an asymmetric buzz through the speaker walks it round, and the gyro closes the loop — drive, coast, stop, shoot, repeat. It needs to be able to slide: hard smooth surface (glass, laminate, a lazy-susan or bearing turntable), case off, volume at maximum and the silent switch off. On carpet, rubber or a soft mat it will not budge — use Hand spin there.

Mode

Auto: the shutter fires by itself every time the phone rotates one step. Just spin steadily.
